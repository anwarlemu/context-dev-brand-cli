# Context.dev principles

Lines marked ASSUMPTION were not answered by the brand owner. They are in force until replaced.

## Belief

The circle is the exact shape of the hole in the square: a missing piece that fits into place.
Hollow circles are the open web, information an agent cannot use yet. Solid circles are context,
the piece that fills the gap so the agent can finish its task. Every pattern, chart and layout is
built from that one circle. If a visual element does not come from the circle, it does not belong.

## Known for

The strongest web data company: scraping, search, research, people and company data, one API.
Better accuracy and a better price than Firecrawl, Exa, Parallel and Tavily, and we prove it with
benchmarks. Add it to Claude Code or Codex and the agent gets better at research and uses fewer tokens.

## Buyer

ASSUMPTION: the engineer or technical founder who owns their product's web data or agent pipeline,
at a B2B company scraping or monitoring large parts of the web. The page must work for someone
spending 100k on tens of millions of requests and for someone pasting one line into their agent.

## Reference screens

Want: firecrawl (consistent, brand aligns with product), resend (tasteful, looks cared for),
modal (consistent, memorable, easy to make brand assets), greptile (memorable, consistent).

Refuse:

- usenotch.ai: yellow and ugly.
- tinyfish.ai: vibe-coded grays.
- Anything that looks generated: gradient blobs, glass, stacked badges, a different hero style per page.

## Non-negotiables

- Nothing that looks vibe-coded. Fewer elements, each one deliberate.
- Surfaces are white, blue `#2563EB` or black. No gradients, glass or water textures.
- Neutrals are for text, borders and quiet panels. Never a gray page, gray card fill or gray brand moment.
- Emphasis in a headline is color (the key phrase in blue), never a heavier weight.
- One primary action per screen: the filled black pill. Every other action is an outline pill.
- Every section is a registry block. Every page starts from a template.
- Claims are provable. A number on the page links to its benchmark or source.
- Doto is the figure face. Use it for: a section's one large stat (`<Stat>`), animated counts and
  step numbers (`<CreditsDotNumber>`), values on charts, and the typed input in the hero demo.
  Never for headings, body, buttons, labels or navigation. Words next to a figure stay in Rethink Sans.
- Dot illustrations are drawn from the circle grid. No stock icons as illustrations, no photos with backgrounds.
- Motion is the dot engine: dots ease between hollow and solid, play only on screen, and rest still with reduced motion. No other animation styles (no parallax, no bouncing cards, no gradient shifts).

## Behavior

- Navigation: top nav on marketing pages (logo, Products, Use Cases, Customers, Pricing, Docs,
  "Book demo", "Start for free"). Footer with four link columns and the dotted wordmark.
- Density: one idea per section. Section heading, one or two lines of muted body, then content.
  Grids of two or three ringed cards. Tables for pricing and credit costs, not cards.
- Default content width: the ruled center column (max 1200px). Sections are separated by rulers.
- Primary action: "Start for free" (filled black pill). Secondary: outline pill in blue.
- Agent path: every page that sells the API offers "Onboard your agent" next to the primary action.
- Destructive actions: none on marketing pages.
- Empty states: marketing pages have no empty state. ASSUMPTION for product surfaces: the empty
  state shows the one next action and a hollow-dot illustration, never a blank panel.

## Core flows

ASSUMPTION, from the homepage and the onboarding form:

1. Visitor lands, understands "web context API for agents", sees the benchmark.
2. Signs up with "Start for free" or pastes the agent setup line ("Onboard your agent").
3. Gets an API key and makes a first request.

## Never change

- The logo, its colorways and clear space (brand guidelines pages 5 to 14).
- `#2563EB`, Rethink Sans, Doto.
- The circle story: hollow is the open web, solid is context.
- ASSUMPTION: legal links (Terms, DPA, Privacy) and SOC badges in the footer.
