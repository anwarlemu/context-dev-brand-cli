## Rules

- The idea: "the circle is the exact shape of the hole in the square, a missing piece that fits
  perfectly into place."
- Minimum size: logomark 16px high, horizontal combination mark 16px high, vertical combination
  mark 32px high.
- Scaling steps shown: 36, 64, 96, 128, 160px.
- Clear space is required on all sides. It is drawn as a guide grid on page 9 with no numeric value.
- Primary background is blue with the white mark. Secondary: blue mark on white, blue mark on
  black, black mark on blue, black mark on white, white mark on grey, white mark on black.
- Do not stretch, recolour, rotate, outline, add shadows, or put the wordmark before the logomark.
- Social profile banner is always white. The profile photo is the logo.
