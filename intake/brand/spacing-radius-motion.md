# Spacing, radius, motion

Source: Figma pages "Brand guidelines (UPDATED)" and "Brand Assets (UPDATED)", read 2026-09-30.

The guidelines define no spacing scale, no radius scale and no motion. Per the brief: none, derive
from reference. What follows is measured from the application designs so there is something to
derive from.
