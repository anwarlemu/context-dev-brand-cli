# Typography

Source: Context.dev Brand Guidelines, Figma pages 19 to 22, and the "Font links" section of the
"Brand Assets (UPDATED)" page. Read 2026-09-30 through the Figma API. Family names, sizes and
line heights are read from live text layers.

## Families

| Role | Family | Source | Licence | Weight in use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Rethink Sans | Google Fonts, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Rethink+Sans | Open Font Licence (to confirm on the specimen page) | 500 for headers, 400 for body |
| Secondary | Doto | Google Fonts, https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Doto | Open Font Licence (to confirm on the specimen page) | 800 only |

Primary: "used for headings, subheadings, and body text across all brand communications and product
design."

Secondary: "reserved for numbers and data, used when a figure needs to stand out and can be shown at
a large size. It is not intended for everyday product use and should appear only in rare cases where
emphasis on the data is needed."

Headers use weight 500. Decision by Anwar, 2026-09-30. This overrides the Figma file, where every
text layer in the guidelines and the application designs is Rethink Sans 400. Body text stays 400.
Emphasis inside a headline is by colour (blue words in a black headline), not by a heavier weight.

itself uses 18px at 150%.

## Not covered

Monospace font for code: the guidelines name none. The website uses IBM Plex Mono for code samples
(`--font-mono`), so that is the working default.
