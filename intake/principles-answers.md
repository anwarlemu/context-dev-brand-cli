## Belief (from the brand guidelines, page 4, "The Idea")

"The Context.dev identity is built from a single idea found in our logo: the circle is the exact
shape of the hole in the square, a missing piece that fits perfectly into place. We carry that idea
through the whole brand.

Hollow circles represent the open web, full of information an agent cannot use yet, and solid
circles represent context, the piece that fills the gap and lets an agent finish its task. Every
pattern, chart and layout is built from that one circle, so the brand tells the same story
everywhere it appears."
