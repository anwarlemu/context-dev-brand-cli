## Observed in the Figma designs (not brand owner answers)

Full list of copy in `intake/figma/README.md`. Patterns, to confirm as rules:

- Sentence case for headlines and buttons.
- American spelling: "colors".
- Product name written "Context.dev", capital C, used as the name rather than "the app".
- Second person: "your agent", "your agent's next step".
- Longest headline is 12 words. Button labels run to 5 words ("Add Context.dev to your agent"),
  over the brief's example limit of 3.
- The tagline is "Web data API for AI Agents". This breaks the brief's default ban on "AI" in
  headlines, so that default should probably be dropped for this brand.
- Some headlines end with a full stop, some do not.
- The announcement label appears four ways: "New:", "New Feature:", "NEW", "NEW!". One needs picking.
- Numbers shown for emphasis use the Doto font, large, and only rarely.
