[Rethink Sans 400 64.0 #000000] 'The Idea'
[Rethink Sans 400 24.0 #000000] 'The Context.dev identity is built from a single idea found in our logo: the circle is the exact shape of the hole in the square, a missing piece that fits perfectly into place. We carry that idea through the whole brand.\n\nHollow circles represent the open web, full of information an agent cannot use yet, and solid circles represent context, the piece that fills the gap and lets an agent finish its task. Every pattern, chart and layout is built from that one circle, so the brand tells the same story everywhere it appears.'
