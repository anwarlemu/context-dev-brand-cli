# Context.dev voice

The YAML header above is read by `context-ds check` (rule `copy-rules`). Everything below is for
people and for `context-ds review`.

Values marked ASSUMPTION in `principles.md` apply here too: word limits and banned words were
inferred from the brand designs, not answered by the brand owner.

## How it sounds

Personable, accessible, flexible, reliable. Somewhat technical, but simple enough that a developer
feels they can add it to Claude Code, improve performance and forget about it. Effortful, not
generated. Every claim can be proven.

- Talk to the builder about their agent: "your agent", "your next feature".
- Say what it does in plain verbs: scrape, search, research, enrich, monitor.
- Short headlines. Put the key phrase in blue, not in bold.
- One idea per sentence. No stacked adjectives.
- Numbers over adjectives: "94% of pages returned as clean, usable content", not "incredibly accurate".
- The four words to keep coming back to: web, agents, accuracy, reliability.

## Mechanics

- Sentence case for headings and buttons.
- American spelling: color, behavior, organization.
- The product is "Context.dev". The API is "the Context.dev API". Not "the app".
- No em dashes. Use a comma, a colon or a full stop.
- Headlines: 12 words or fewer. Subheads: 24 or fewer. Buttons: 5 or fewer.
- Announcements start with "New:".

## Lines that work

From the brand designs and the homepage (ASSUMPTION: the brand owner has not picked these):

- "Paste one line. Your agent sets up Context.dev itself."
- "Give your agent the web data it needs to finish a task."
- "Accurate data for your agent's next step."
- "One API gives your agent the whole web as context."
- "Cheaper, better, faster, stronger at any scale."

## Lines that do not

ASSUMPTION examples of what to avoid, written to the rules above:

- "Unlock the power of seamless web data." Two banned phrases and says nothing.
- "The revolutionary, next-generation, AI-powered scraping platform." Adjective stack.
- "Scraping, reimagined" written with an em dash. Banned character, and no claim.
- "Build Faster With Context.Dev" Title case and a misspelled name.
- "Incredibly accurate results." Adjective where a number should be.
