# Review rubric

`context-ds review` sends this rubric, `principles.md`, `voice.md`, the screenshots and the git diff
to the editor model. Scores are 0 to 10. 7 is shippable with fixes, 9 is exemplary, 5 is generic.

## Hierarchy

One message per page, one idea per section. The eye lands on the headline, then the key phrase in
blue, then the single filled primary action. Section headings, body and actions keep the same order
on every section. Nothing competes with the primary action.

## Coherence across the flow

Every section is built from the circle: hollow dots for the open web, solid dots for context. The
page reads as one system: same card ring, same pill, same rulers, same spacing rhythm. No section
looks like it came from another site (glass, gradients, water, stock illustrations, gray fills).

## Brand voice

Sentence case, plain verbs, numbers over adjectives, no banned words. Speaks to the builder about
their agent. Claims are provable and point to a benchmark or source. Every headline passes the
"could a competitor say this" test; if Firecrawl could say it word for word, it is generic.

## Detail quality

Alignment to the ruled column, consistent card padding, dot grids aligned to their pitch, no widows
in headlines, no orphaned single cards in a grid, empty and hover states considered, nothing clipped
at any viewport. Mobile is designed, not collapsed.

## Accessibility

Contrast meets AA for the pairs in build/contrast-report.md. Focus is visible. Headings are in order.
Dot illustrations have text alternatives or are decorative. Touch targets are 44px on mobile.

## Punch list rules

- Name the region (section name and viewport) and quote the exact copy.
- Say what is dead, generic or off-principle, and the concrete fix.
- No praise. If a section is fine, leave it out.
