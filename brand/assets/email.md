# Emails

The brand has no email designs of its own (the website repository has none at 2f4c4559). `email` derives them strictly
from the tokens and the site's parts: the frame gray ground (`bg-frame`), a white card with a hairline (`neutral-10`) and
the card radius, headings in the brand sans at 500 and -0.02em in the brand blue, body in `text-default`, captions in
`text-muted`, the pill button in `brand` with white words, and the Doto label as a monospace chip (mail clients do not
load Doto; Courier New stands in). Fonts fall back to the system sans where Rethink Sans is not installed.
