# Context.dev: what good looks like

Read by `context-ds review` every time, next to the reference screenshots in `brand/reference/`.

## The idea

The logomark is a square with a circular hole and the circle that fits it. Hollow circles are the open web,
information an agent cannot use yet; solid circles are context, the piece that lets it finish. The dots in the
brand come from that circle, and they are a supporting texture: they sit at the edges of a hero or inside an
illustration, never behind text and never as decoration everywhere.

## The site (marketing pages)

- A white page, black type, blue (#2563EB) only as an accent: the closing phrase of a headline, outline buttons,
  links, the soft 8px ring around cards, the dot streams at the hero's edges, a stat. No gradients, glass or glows.
- Rethink Sans at weight 400 for everything, headings tight (-0.035em). Hierarchy is size and color, never bold.
  Doto only for a figure that needs emphasis (a counter, a step number).
- The page sits in a ruled column: hairlines down the column edges, a full-bleed rule between sections with a
  small solid node where it meets each edge. Section headings are left aligned with their description below.
- Cards are white with a hairline, inside a soft blue ring of the same radius, in grids of two or three.
- One filled black pill per screen (the main action); every other action is an outline blue pill.
- The hero is centered: eyebrow, title with its key phrase in blue, one line of subtitle, the two pills, then its
  visual in a ring frame (or a small demo in the blue panel). Thin dot streams run in from its edges.
- Restraint: fewer elements, each deliberate. Nothing that looks generated: stacked badges, blobs, a different
  style per section, dots across whole sections, heavy shadows.

## The product (dashboard, admin, any app people work in)

- Calm, dense, quick: a tool used daily. The canvas (#fafafa) with white cards whose hairline is part of the
  shadow. A 15px root; the main UI text is 12.6px; page titles 21px at weight 450; nothing bold.
- Navigation is neutral: the selected item is a gray wash, never blue. Links are neutral too.
- One blue button per page or form (the main action); others outline or ghost; destructive actions outlined red.
- Tables on a card, a faint gray header band in sentence case, light row dividers, tabular numbers.
- Hover is a wash, not a lift. Clickable cards get a 4px translucent blue ring on hover.
- Blue marks what matters: the main action, focus, selection, the switch, the brand glyphs, chart totals.

## Always

- Keep what each screen is for: its main action stays the most prominent thing, its key information easy to scan.
- Keep every word, link, image and element. Only how it looks changes.
- Text contrast at least 4.5:1; touch targets at least 44px on phones; visible focus on every control.
- Motion is small and fast (150 to 250ms, ease-out), never ease-in, and stills with reduced motion.

## Voice (for any text the agent must write, such as an alt text)

Plain, technical, short. Sentence case, American spelling, no em dashes. Never "seamless", "revolutionary",
"cutting-edge", "unleash", "supercharge", "next-generation", "world-class", "leverage", "elevate".
